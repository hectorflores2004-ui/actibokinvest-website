How to invest in El Salvador

A step-by-step guide to setting up and launching a company, with the documents typically required.

1

Define the structure

Before any filing, the legal form and the investment are decided.

  • Corporation (Sociedad Anónima de Capital Variable, S.A. de C.V.)
  • Limited liability company (S. de R.L. de C.V.)
  • Branch of a foreign company
  • Sole-owner limited liability company
2

Incorporate the company

The public incorporation deed is executed before a notary and registered with the Commercial Registry (CNR).

  • Shareholder ID (DUI or passport)
  • Powers of attorney, apostilled when an agent acts from abroad
  • Company name, share capital and address
  • Business license (matrícula) and establishments
3

Tax registrations

Registration with the Ministry of Finance and issuance of tax numbers.

  • NIT (Tax Identification Number)
  • NRC (VAT taxpayer registration)
  • Electronic invoicing (DTE) authorization
  • Appointment of accountant and, if applicable, tax auditor
4

Permits and licenses

Depending on the business activity, additional registrations and permits are required.

  • Registration with the municipal office
  • Health, environmental or sector permits as applicable
  • Employee enrollment with ISSS and AFP when hiring
  • Visas or residence permits if the investor relocates
5

Operate in compliance

Once operating, the company has recurring obligations.

  • Formal bookkeeping and financial statements
  • Monthly filings (VAT, withholdings) and annual income tax return
  • Payroll and contributions
  • Audit and business license renewal

Frequently asked questions


Can a foreigner own 100% of a company?

Generally, foreigners may invest in and own companies in El Salvador with treatment similar to nationals. Some activities have specific restrictions; we review your case.

Do I need to travel to El Salvador to incorporate?

Not always. With properly granted and apostilled powers of attorney, an agent can complete the filings. We tell you which documents to prepare in your country.

How long does it take and how much does it cost?

It depends on the company type, capital and activity. We provide a written estimate after learning about your project.

Do I need an accountant?

Yes. Companies must keep formal accounting and meet periodic tax obligations, supported by an authorized accountant.
